# Privacy policy — Xonix32

Xonix32 does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

- No network access: the game is fully offline.
- No analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind.
- No account, no sign-in.
- The only data written is the local high-score table
  (name-as-entered and score), stored in a `HiScores.dat` file inside
  the app's own private storage on your device. It never leaves the
  device. Deleting the app deletes it.

In the words of the original 1998 readme: "Like all good and decent
software, Xonix32 does not store anything outside of its own
directory."
