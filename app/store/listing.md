## Full description

Xonix32 is a faithful mobile port of the classic 1997/98 Windows arcade
game by Shawn A. VanNess — same six colors, same chunky pixels, same
rules, same feel.

Steer the Xoni across the black sea to claim territory. Close off a
region and it floods with blue — but any area holding a white diamond
stays wild. Claim 75% of the field to clear the level.

Watch out for:
• White diamonds — deadly if they touch your trail
• Black diamonds — patrol the land you've claimed
• Yellow wipers — erase your walls and your unfinished trails

Features:
• The original gameplay, ported line by line from the 1998 GPL source
• Swipe anywhere to steer; tap to pause
• Adjustable game speed (10–50 FPS) and starting level (1–20), just
  like the original's dialogs
• Top-10 high-score table, stored in the original HiScores.dat format
• The original splash screen, message bitmaps, and icon
• No ads, no tracking, no network access — like all good and decent
  software, Xonix32 keeps nothing outside its own directory
